## The code (Apache-2.0)

You may:

- **use** it, for any purpose, including at work and in commercial products;
- **change** it;
- **share** it, changed or unchanged;
- **sell** it, or products that include it.

When you share a copy, changed or not, you must:

- **include the licence** (the `LICENSE` file);
- **keep the `NOTICE` file** and its attribution, and keep the copyright notices in the files;
- **mark the files you changed**, with a clear note in each one saying that you changed it.

**Patents:** everyone who contributes code grants you a licence to any of their patents that the contribution needs. That grant ends for anyone who sues claiming the code infringes their patent.

The code comes **as is**, with no warranty: if it fails, nobody owes you for it.

## The book (CC BY-ND 4.0)

You may:

- **share the book freely**: copy it, print it, post it, hand it out, even commercially;
- as long as you **give credit** (Build It Safe, by Forsaken Roads®), **link to the licence**, and **share it unchanged**.

You may not:

- **publish an edited version**, or a shortened, rearranged or "improved" one, without permission. Quoting a passage with credit is fine; republishing a changed book is not.

**Translations** count as changed versions, so they need permission. We would like to hear from you: ask by a direct message to [@Forsaken_Roads](https://x.com/Forsaken_Roads) on X.

## Forks

You may fork the code. A fork must:

- **say clearly that it is a fork** of Build It Safe by Forsaken Roads®, with a link to the original;
- **keep the `NOTICE` file**, which points to the official project;
- use its own name, not one that suggests it is official.
