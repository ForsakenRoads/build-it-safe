# Part II. The fundamentals {#part-2 .part}

::: {.part-intro}
Part I showed what goes wrong. This part shows how to prevent it.

Each chapter starts with one idea, a mental model, that explains a whole family of mistakes. Then it shows the practice: what to set up and what to check. Then it lists the mistakes AI assistants commonly make on that topic, because you will be reviewing their work. Each chapter ends with a short "Do this" checklist.

You do not need to read these chapters in order. If you are about to launch, read {{ch:ch-secrets}}, {{ch:ch-doors}} and {{ch:ch-money}} first.
:::
