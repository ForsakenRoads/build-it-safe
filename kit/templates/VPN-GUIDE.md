# VPN guide

A VPN (virtual private network) sends your internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel to a server run by the VPN company. It hides what you do from the local network, such as café Wi-Fi, and moves trust to the VPN company. It does not protect leaked keys, weak passwords, open databases or malicious packages.

The manual's chapter **"Your connection"** explains when a VPN helps, when it does not, and how to choose one. Read it there:

- PDF: `manual/dist/security-primer.pdf` (the chapter "Your connection", in Part II)
